# FarmGate Hub — Investor Pitch Deck 📊

*Transforming Smallholder Tomato Incomes via Decentralized Micro-Cold Storage and Direct Wholesale Matching.*
*Originating from Group 4 Student Report "Improving Farmers' Income".*

---

## Slide 1: Executive Summary & The Problem
- **The Reality:** 86% of Indian farmers are smallholders (<2 hectares).
- **The Shock:** Tomatoes face up to 25% post-harvest spoilage and price collapse down to ₹3–₹4/kg during peak flush.
- **The Share:** Farmers capture only **33%** of the consumer rupee; the rest vanishes in intermediary markups and transit damage.

---

## Slide 2: Why Centralized Packhouses Fail
- Centralized mega-packhouses (500+ tonnes) are located 30–60 km from farm gates.
- High transportation barriers and delayed aggregation force distress yard sales.
- **Our Model:** Hyper-local, modular, 10-tonne village-level micro-hubs located within a 5 km walking radius.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution — FarmGate Hub
- **Decentralized Solar-Hybrid Cool Pods:** 10-tonne capacity with Phase Change Material (PCM) 72-hour thermal backup at 11.5°C.
- **Fast 30-Second Grading:** Digital scale capture and USDA/AGMARK inspection with QR thermal labels.
- **Collective Load Building:** Aggregating micro-lots into wholesale consignments for pre-verified buyers.
- **24-Hour SLA Payouts:** Direct UPI settlement into the farmer's bank account with optional 60% produce advances.

---

## Slide 4: The 4-Step Workflow
1. **Intake & Weighing:** Farmer brings produce; digital scale records tare weight; 30-second inspection assigns Grade A, B, or C.
2. **Cool Pod Safe Holding:** Produce pre-cooled to 11.5°C; farmer receives guaranteed FPO floor price protection (₹11/kg).
3. **Algorithmic Matching:** Software matches graded lots to verified buyer demand with 100% escrow deposits.
4. **24-Hour Settlement:** Instant UPI payout upon truck dispatch; reconciled double-entry ledger eliminates middlemen cuts.
