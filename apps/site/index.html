Section 4.1 Root Cause

Why Smallholders Lose 30% Before Reaching the Market

Indian horticulture hemorrhages ₹38,600 Crore each year across four interlocking structural failure points:

01 / DECAY

Physical Spoilage

Lack of immediate pre-cooling in field heat triggers rapid degradation. 15% to 25% of fresh tomatoes rot within 48 hours.

02 / OPACITY

Price Asymmetry

Daily mandi price swings leave smallholders blind. Without holding power, farmers are forced into distress sales at ₹9.00/kg.

03 / MARGIN CUTS

Intermediary Rents

Multiple tiers of commission agents and brokers extract margins, leaving the farmer with only 33% of the final consumer rupee.

04 / LIQUIDITY

Delayed Liquidity

Traditional credit cycles take 15 to 45 days. Immediate cash needs force farmers into high-interest local debt.

Operational Blueprint

From Farm Gate to Wholesale Buyer in 4 Disciplined Steps

A streamlined, repeatable 24-hour cycle verified on the Kolar Hub pilot:

08:00 AM • STEP 1

Intake & Grading

Produce is weighed on a digital platform scale. A 30-second checklist grades quality (A/B/C) and prints a thermal QR traceability label.

09:00 AM • STEP 2

Cool Pod Pre-Cooling

Lots are loaded into the 10-tonne solar cool pod at 11.5°C. Immediate field-heat removal extends marketable shelf-life up to 72 hours.

14:00 PM • STEP 3

Collective Load Match

The algorithmic demand engine aggregates micro-lots into wholesale orders (e.g. 500 kg Grade A for Annapurna Retail @ ₹14.00/kg).

18:00 PM • STEP 4

24h UPI Settlement

As soon as the collective truck dispatches, automated direct UPI transfers disburse net proceeds. 100% on-time SLA guarantee.
